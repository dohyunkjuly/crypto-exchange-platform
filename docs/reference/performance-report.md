- **Orders per second, one partition**: The capacity of one market partition; the exchange scales by adding partitions, each its own thread.
- **Order to fill, through the website**: The whole path a click takes: HTTP, outbox, Kafka, the engine, Kafka, the socket.
- **Place an order over the API**: Signature check, eligibility, funds lock, outbox write and commit, in one round trip.
- **Market sockets served at once**: One web-bff, one laptop as the client. Horizontal: more web-bff instances behind nginx, each a full fan-out.
- **Memory per resting order**: Falls as fixed costs spread out, from about 680 bytes at 5,000 orders: ten million resting orders is about 5.3 GB of book.
- **Back after a crash**: A restart replays at most this much; a snapshot every 30,000 commands keeps it bounded.
